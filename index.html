The five spaceflight hazards

Space changes the context

Radiation

Energetic particles can affect tissues and raise long-term health uncertainty. Neutron radiation is a major, biologically harmful part of in-station exposure.

Isolation & confinement

Distance from familiar people and limited private space can affect sleep and mood.

Distance from Earth

Communication delay means crews need useful, understandable onboard guidance.

Gravity fields

Microgravity shifts fluids and unloads bones and muscles; transitions add new strain.

Hostile closed environment

Cabin air, noise, workload, and constrained resources shape daily wellbeing.
